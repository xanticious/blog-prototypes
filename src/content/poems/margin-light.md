A note left in the margin of a long *evening*, for the reader who stays past the point of being **sensible**.

> Keep the lamp until the paragraph ends.

:::verse
The lamp is a small moon
tied to the table's edge.
I turn a page
and the margin keeps the hour.

Somewhere a kettle ticks.
Somewhere the house forgets
its list of unfinished rooms.

I underline a sentence
only because the voice
sounded like someone
telling the truth sideways.

Outside, the street is ink.
Inside, one warm rectangle
and the patience to stay
until the paragraph ends.

Close the book.
The lamp stays on a moment,
faithful as a dog
who has not been told
the story is over.
:::
