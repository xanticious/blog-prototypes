I kept both of them open, which is a bad way to read and a good way to notice the fight. They are not a series. Nobody walks out of Thornfield and onto the moor. They are a related shelf: two novels, two sisters, one year.

Charlotte gives Jane the whole book. Emily will not let Catherine hold the microphone. **One novel trusts a voice. The other trusts a house full of versions.** I wrote “leave” in the margin of *Jane Eyre* and “stay and wreck it” in *Wuthering Heights*.

I loved it when ||Jane walked out||, and I flinched when ||Heathcliff spent the second half on the children||. Read them together if you want the argument, not a sequel.
