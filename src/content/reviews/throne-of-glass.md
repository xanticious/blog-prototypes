Celaena is the coolest most fearsome heroine. She's sassy and fun.

This book is about Celaena Sardothian who the Prince of Adarlan enters into a competition to become Adarlan's Assassin, even though Celaena is already known as Adarlan's Assassin, so naturally the competition is just a formality. It's not all fun and games though. Something is killing the competitors, and if Celaena doesn't figure out who's behind it, she might be next on the hit list.

It is so fun! You should read it! I'd recommend this to anyone!
