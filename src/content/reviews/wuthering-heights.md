Nobody in this book is having a nice time, including me, and I kept reading anyway. The frame — a tenant, a housekeeper, a story told at a remove — should make it cozier. It doesn’t. **Nelly judges everyone and still tells you the worst.**

I wrote “too much” in the margin about once a chapter. I meant it as a complaint until the middle, when I loved it when ||Catherine said she *was* Heathcliff, and then married Edgar anyway||. The sentence is unhinged. It is also the clearest thing anyone says.

The second generation almost talked me into hope. Almost. I closed it glad I do not live on those moors and a little sad that the wind in the last pages is the only kindness.
