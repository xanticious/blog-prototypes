I started this on a train and nearly missed my stop during Walton’s letters, which is embarrassing, because almost nothing has happened yet except weather and ambition. **Victor is already unbearable, and the book knows it.**

The creature’s chapter is the one I slowed down for. He learns to read by watching a family through a crack in the wall, and it is tender right up until it isn’t. I hated it when ||Victor destroyed the companion he had promised to make||. I also understood it, which is worse.

*Frankenstein* is a science story that keeps turning into a grief story. I finished it annoyed at everyone and sorry for the being with no name.
