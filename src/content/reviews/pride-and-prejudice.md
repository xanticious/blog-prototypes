The first assembly is *meaner* than I remembered. Elizabeth decides she has Darcy’s whole character by the time the music stops, and she is so pleased about it that I started taking his side out of spite. **She is quick, and the quickness keeps her wrong for a long time.**

I put the book down after the letter. I had to. It is the chapter where the book turns around and looks at you. I loved it when ||Elizabeth and Darcy finally got together||, though by then I mostly loved that she had to admit she had misread him.

Charlotte’s line — “I am not romantic, you know” — is still the one I keep. Practical, a little cold, and more honest than the ballroom.
